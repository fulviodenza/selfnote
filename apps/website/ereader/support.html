Selfnote eReader support

A local-first EPUB reader for iPhone and iPad. Your books stay on your device, and your highlights go to a Selfnote server you run yourself, if and when you want them to.

Get help

Everything goes to the same place, the issue tracker, and is answered in public so the next person with the same question finds the answer:

  • Something is broken, or you want a feature: open an issue. Please say which device and iOS version you are on, and what you did just before the problem.
  • A specific book will not open: include the book's file size and where you got it from. Do not attach the book itself unless it is freely redistributable.
  • Highlights are not reaching Selfnote: see "Not sent" below first, since the message after it names the actual cause.

The app is open source. If you would rather read the code than file a report, it is at apps/ereader.

Reading

Which files can I read?

EPUB only. PDFs and audiobooks are not supported. Books with DRM, meaning anything bought from a store that locks its files, cannot be opened by any app other than that store's own.

How do I add a book?

Tap Add an EPUB and pick the file. It can come from Files, iCloud Drive, or any other source the iOS document picker can reach. The app copies the file into its own storage, so the book keeps working even if you later move or delete the original.

How do I turn pages?

Tap the left third of the page to go back, the right third to go forward. On an iPad in landscape you get two pages side by side, one page upright.

How do I highlight a passage?

Select the text. The highlight is saved as soon as the selection ends. Tap an existing highlight to see it again, with the option to remove it.

Does it remember my place?

Yes. Each book reopens on the page you left. The percentage in the top bar is measured across the whole book rather than the chapter you are in, so it is built once per book on first open and reused from then on.

Sending highlights to your Selfnote

How do I connect my instance?

Open Send highlights to Selfnote, enter your Selfnote address (just the host is fine, notes.example.com, and https is assumed), and sign in with the email and password of your Selfnote account. That is the only time you type the password. The app immediately trades it for a personal access token called "Selfnote eReader", keeps only that token, in the iOS keychain, and discards everything else.

You need a Selfnote server for this. If you do not have one, the self-hosting guide covers a Helm chart for Kubernetes or three processes and a Postgres on any box.

Where do the highlights go?

Onto a page you choose, per book. Open a book, tap the page target in the top bar, and pick an existing page or create a new one; the picker suggests "<book> notes", which is what most people would type anyway. From then on every passage you highlight is appended to that page.

It is per book rather than one page for everything on purpose. Two books in one page is nobody's filing system.

Can I change or stop it later?

Yes. The picker has Stop sending to "<page>", and picking a different page moves future highlights there. Highlights already written to a page stay on it; they are yours to edit or delete in Selfnote like any other note.

What happens when I highlight with no signal?

It is saved locally and queued. The backlog is sent the next time you highlight something, the next time you open that book, or as soon as you pick a page for it. Each highlight carries an id generated once when you made it, so the server ignores anything it has already written and re-sending the whole queue is the normal way this recovers rather than an error.

It says "Not sent"

The text after it is the reason, passed through from your own server. The usual causes:

  • Your server is unreachable from where you are. The highlight is safe locally and will go out on the next attempt.
  • Your token was revoked in Selfnote. Reconnect from Send highlights to Selfnote.
  • Your Selfnote is older than the highlight ingest endpoint. Sending needs a server that accepts POST /integrations/highlights (issue #67). Update your instance.

Nothing is lost in any of these cases. An unsent highlight stays unsent and keeps being retried.

Does it sync between my devices?

No. Highlights travel one way, from a device to your server, so this is not a way to carry reading position from an iPhone to an iPad.

Do I have to connect anything?

No, and that is deliberate. Reading, highlighting, page turns, and reading position all work with no connection, and disconnecting leaves every one of them working.

Your data

Where is it?

In a SQLite database inside the app's own container on your device: the books you imported, your highlights, and your reading position for each. Nothing leaves the device unless you connect an instance, and then it goes only to the address you typed. There is no Selfnote cloud, no analytics, and no third-party SDK. Scripts inside EPUB files are disabled, so a book cannot run code or call home either.

The full policy is on the privacy page.

How do I get my highlights out?

Connect an instance and they are already out, on a page in your own workspace, exportable like any other Selfnote page. A standalone export for people who do not run Selfnote is not built; if you want one, say so on the issue tracker.

How do I remove a single book?

Not possible in the current version. You can remove individual highlights by tapping them.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Its container goes with it, including every book, highlight, reading position, and the stored token. Anything already sent to your Selfnote stays there, because it is your server; delete those pages in Selfnote if you want them gone.
