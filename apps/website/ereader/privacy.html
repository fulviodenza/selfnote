Selfnote eReader privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026. In one line: the developer of this app collects nothing about you, and the only place your reading can ever be sent is a server you operate yourself.

What the app stores on your device

  • the EPUB files you import,
  • your highlights and any notes attached to them,
  • your reading position and cached page index for each book,
  • if you connect a Selfnote server, its address, your email address, and an access token for it, held in the iOS keychain.

All of it lives in the app's own private container. Your Selfnote password is never stored. You type it once when connecting, the app immediately exchanges it for an access token named "Selfnote eReader", and keeps only that token. You can revoke the token from your Selfnote account at any time without changing your password.

What the app sends, and where

Until you connect a Selfnote server, the app makes no network requests at all. It has no accounts of its own, no servers of its own, and nothing to sign up for.

If you choose to connect one, the app sends, only to the address you typed and only over that connection:

  • your email address and password, once, to sign in and obtain a token,
  • the text of the highlights you make, any note attached to them, their position in the book, and the book's title and author, to the page you picked for that book,
  • requests to list or create pages in your workspace, so you can choose where highlights land.

That server is yours. Selfnote is self-hosted software: there is no Selfnote cloud, no account with us, and no intermediary. The developer of this app operates no service that your data passes through and has no access to your instance. What happens to the data once it reaches your server is governed by how you run that server.

Highlights travel one way, from your device to your server. The app never downloads your notes back.

What the app never does

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting, no device identifiers, no third-party SDKs of any kind. Nothing about what you read, highlight, or import is shared with anyone, and nothing is sold. Scripts embedded in EPUB files are disabled, so a book cannot make network requests on its own behalf either.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

You can remove any highlight from inside the app, and disconnect your Selfnote instance at any time, which deletes the stored token and leaves every reading feature working. Deleting the app removes its entire container: every book, highlight, reading position, and credential.

Highlights already sent to your Selfnote stay on your server, because it is your server. Delete those pages in Selfnote to remove them, and revoke the "Selfnote eReader" token from your account page if you want to be sure nothing can be added again.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date. Selfnote is open source, so this is checkable rather than merely promised: the reader is at apps/ereader and the network code is one small file, src/selfnote.ts.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue. For anything about using the app, see the support page.
