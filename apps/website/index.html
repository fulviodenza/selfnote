Your notes, on your server.

Selfnote is a private workspace for the notes that matter: a fast Notion-style editor, real-time collaboration, and offline writing that just works. It lives on your own server, so your thinking stays yours, on web, desktop, and mobile.

Free and open source · web, desktop, and mobile · your notes never leave your server

Two people can type in the same sentence. One of them can be offline.

Write together and watch each other's cursors move, or write alone on a plane. When you come back online, everyone's edits merge cleanly, without a conflict dialog, ever. And nothing is lost along the way: every page keeps its full history, so you can preview any past version and bring it back.

Write

  • Block editorHeadings, todos, tables, code, callouts, images, and files, the same on every device.
  • Slash commandsType / to insert blocks, link notes, or run AI actions without leaving the keyboard.
  • TabsOpen pages like browser tabs and jump between them; your open set is right where you left it.
  • Version historyNamed restore points on a timeline; preview any past state and restore it with one click.

Connect

  • Backlinks & graphLink notes to notes, see what links here, and explore your workspace as a 3D graph you can orbit.
  • Tasks & calendarPromote any page to a task with a due date; see it on your Google, Apple, or Outlook calendar.
  • SharingShare a page with a read or write link, watch how often it's viewed, and revoke it any time.
  • Obsidian importBring your whole vault: folders become nested pages, images come along, callouts stay callouts.

Own

  • Your server, your notesSelfnote runs where you decide. No account with us, no tracking, nothing phones home.
  • Offline-firstNotes live on your device too; read and write with no connection and let sync catch up later.
  • Files & mediaPDFs, images, and videos live next to your notes and preview in place, with one tidy view of everything you've attached.
  • AI on your termsThe assistant only exists if you turn it on, and it can run entirely on your own hardware.

One design, three clients.

Web, desktop, and mobile share the same "Ink & Paper" design, the same editor, and the same sync, so a note feels like the same note everywhere, in light and dark.

AI that answers to you.

AI is off until you turn it on, and you choose what powers it. Once you do, the ✦ Assist panel appears on every device: a chat that knows the note you're editing, one-tap actions like Summarize and Extract action items, and AI edits you review as a proposal and approve before they touch a page.
