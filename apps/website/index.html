Your notes, on your server.

Selfnote is a self-hosted workspace for people who want Notion-style editing without handing their thinking to someone else's cloud. Real-time collaboration, offline-first sync, and AI that runs against a model you control — on web, desktop, and mobile.

Download for Android Self-host in 10 minutes

Open source · one Helm chart or a Kubernetes operator · bring your own Postgres box

Two people can type in the same sentence. One of them can be offline.

Every document is a CRDT (Yjs on the clients, its Rust port yrs on the server), so edits merge by structure, not by timestamp. Go offline for a day, keep writing, reconnect — your changes and everyone else's converge without a conflict dialog, ever. The server persists the update stream to Postgres and compacts snapshots, so documents survive restarts and time-travel stays possible.

Write

  • Block editorThe same BlockNote editor on every client — headings, todos, tables, code, callouts, images.
  • Slash commandsType / to insert blocks, link notes, or run AI actions without leaving the keyboard.
  • Page treeNested pages with rename, archive, and search across the workspace.
  • Version historyNamed restore points on a timeline; preview any past state and restore it as a new edit — history is never destroyed.

Connect

  • Backlinks & graphLink notes to notes, see what links here, and explore the workspace as a 3D graph you can orbit.
  • Tasks & calendarPromote any page to a task with a due date; subscribe to your workspace as an iCal feed in Google, Apple, or Outlook.
  • SharingPublic read or write links backed by short-lived room tokens, with view counts so you know when to revoke.
  • Obsidian importBring an existing Markdown vault in whole.

Own

  • Self-hosted, fullyTwo small Rust services and Postgres. Nothing is hardcoded — native apps ask which instance to connect to on first launch.
  • Offline-firstIndexedDB on web, SQLite on mobile; the app is complete without a connection.
  • AI on your termsThe Assist panel exists only when your server configures a provider — and it can be a model on your own box.
  • Kubernetes-nativeA Helm chart today, a Selfnote operator CRD for one-command installs and gated upgrades.

One design, three clients.

Web, desktop (Tauri), and mobile (Expo) share the same "Ink & Paper" design system, the same editor, and the same sync core — a note feels like the same note everywhere, in light and dark.

AI that answers to your server.

AI is off by default and server-owned. Configure one provider on your API and the ✦ Assist panel appears on every client automatically — a streaming chat grounded in the note you're editing, inline actions like Summarize and Extract action items, and staged AI edits you approve as diffs before they touch a document.

And it works in the other direction too: a bundled MCP server lets Claude — in the CLI, Desktop, or claude.ai — save a summary of any conversation straight into your notes and hand you back the link.
