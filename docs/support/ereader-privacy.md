# Selfnote eReader privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Selfnote eReader does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

## What the app stores

Everything the app stores, it stores only on your device, inside the app's own private
container:

- the EPUB files you import,
- your highlights and any notes attached to them,
- your reading position and cached page index for each book.

## What the app sends

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers,
no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Nobody,
including the developer, can see what you read or what you highlight.

Scripts embedded in EPUB files are disabled, so a book cannot make network requests on
its own behalf either.

## Data you remove

You can remove any highlight from inside the app. Deleting the app removes its entire
container, including every book, highlight, and reading position. No copy is kept
anywhere else.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds optional synchronization to a Selfnote server you control, this
policy will be updated before that version ships, and such a feature would be off unless
you turn it on.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [open an issue](https://github.com/fulviodenza/selfnote/issues).
